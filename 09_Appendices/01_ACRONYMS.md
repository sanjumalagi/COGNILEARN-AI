# 13. Architectural Principle

Throughout the CogniLearn AI documentation, the following principle remains constant:

> **Educational Intelligence drives Teaching Intelligence.**

Educational Intelligence determines:

- What should be taught.
- When it should be taught.
- Why it should be taught.
- Which teaching strategy should be applied.

The AI Service Layer and Large Language Model generate instructional content based solely on the structured teaching context produced by Educational Intelligence.
