# Core Architectural Principles

The following principles are referenced throughout the documentation:

- Educational Intelligence drives Teaching Intelligence.
- Teaching Intelligence prepares instructional context.
- The AI Service Layer abstracts external AI providers.
- Large Language Models generate instructional content only.
- Educational reasoning remains independent of AI provider implementation.
- Configuration is externalized for portability.
- Security is implemented throughout the software lifecycle.
- Modular architecture supports maintainability and scalability.
