# 1. Introduction

Welcome to **CogniLearn AI**, an intelligent adaptive learning platform designed to personalize your learning experience. Unlike traditional e-learning systems, CogniLearn AI continuously analyzes your learning progress and provides customized assessments, recommendations, and AI-assisted instructional support.

This manual explains how to use the platform effectively, from creating an account to monitoring your learning progress.

---

# 2. Intended Users

This manual is intended for:

- Students
- Learners
- Self-paced learners
- Engineering students
- Educational institutions using CogniLearn AI

No technical knowledge is required.

---

To use CogniLearn AI, ensure the following:

### Supported Devices

- Desktop Computer
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Smartphone

### Supported Browsers

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari

### Internet Connection

A stable internet connection is recommended for:

- Adaptive assessments
- AI Tutor interactions
- Progress synchronization

# 4. Accessing the Platform

Open your preferred web browser and navigate to the platform URL provided by your institution or administrator.

Example:

```
https://cognilearn.ai
```

The Home Page provides options to:

- Register
- Log In
- Learn about the platform
- Access help resources

---

# 5. Creating a New Account

### Step 1

Select **Register**.

### Step 2

Enter:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Password

### Step 3

Click **Create Account**.

If registration is successful, you will receive confirmation and can proceed to the login page.

---

# 6. Logging In

Enter:

- Registered Email Address
- Password

Click **Login**.

After successful authentication, you will be redirected to your personalized dashboard.

---

# 7. Dashboard Overview

The learner dashboard provides quick access to all major features.

Main sections include:

- Learning Progress
- Adaptive Assessments
- Personalized Recommendations
- AI Tutor
- Analytics
- Learning History
- Profile Settings

The dashboard is personalized according to your learning progress.

---

# 8. Starting an Adaptive Assessment

To begin an assessment:

1. Select **Assessments**.
2. Choose a course.
3. Select a module or topic.
4. Click **Start Assessment**.

The system generates questions based on your current knowledge level.

During the assessment:

- Read each question carefully.
- Select the most appropriate answer.
- Submit your response.
- Continue until the assessment is complete.

Question difficulty may adapt according to your performance.

---

# 9. Viewing Assessment Results

After completing an assessment, the system displays:

- Overall Score
- Topic Mastery
- Ability Estimate
- Correct Answers
- Explanations
- Strengths
- Areas for Improvement

These results are used to personalize future learning activities.

---

# 10. Personalized Learning Recommendations

After each assessment, CogniLearn AI recommends learning activities such as:

- Topics to review
- New concepts to study
- Suggested learning order
- Practice opportunities

Recommendations are generated automatically based on your learning progress.

---

# 11. Using the AI Tutor

The AI Tutor provides instructional assistance.

To ask a question:

1. Open **AI Tutor**.
2. Type your question.
3. Submit your request.

Example:

```
Explain Binary Search with an example.
```

The AI Tutor provides explanations, examples, and guidance based on the instructional context prepared by the Educational Intelligence layer.

---

# 12. Viewing Learning Analytics

The Analytics section displays:

- Assessment History
- Learning Progress
- Topic Mastery
- Course Completion
- Learning Trends
- Recent Activities

These analytics help you monitor your improvement over time.

# 13. Managing Your Profile

Under **Profile Settings**, you can:

- Update your name.
- Change your password.
- Update your email address (if permitted).
- Review account information.
- Log out.

Keeping your profile information current helps maintain account security.

# 14. Notifications

The platform may display notifications for:

- Completed assessments.
- New recommendations.
- Course updates.

Review notifications regularly to stay informed.

---

# 15. Logging Out

To end your session:

1. Select your profile menu.
2. Click **Logout**.

Always log out when using shared or public devices.

---

# 16. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Why are my questions different from another student's?

Assessments are adaptive. The platform selects questions according to each learner's current knowledge level.

---

### Q2. Can I retake an assessment?

Yes. Availability depends on your instructor or institutional policy.

---

### Q3. Does the AI Tutor determine my grades?

No.

The AI Tutor provides instructional support only. Assessment evaluation and learning decisions are managed by the Educational Intelligence layer.

---

### Q4. Why did my recommendations change?

Recommendations are updated automatically after new assessments and learning activities.

---

### Q5. What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the **Forgot Password** option on the login page or contact your system administrator.

---

# 17. Best Practices

For the best learning experience:

- Complete assessments honestly.
- Review explanations carefully.
- Follow recommended learning paths.
- Practice regularly.
- Monitor your analytics.
- Use the AI Tutor to clarify concepts.
- Maintain a stable internet connection during assessments.

---

# 18. Privacy and Security

To protect your account:

- Use a strong password.
- Do not share your login credentials.
- Log out after each session on shared devices.
- Report suspicious account activity immediately.
- Keep your browser updated.

---

# 19. Getting Help

If you experience problems:

- Review the FAQ.
- Contact your instructor.
- Contact the system administrator.
- Refer to institutional support resources.

Provide details such as screenshots, error messages, and the steps that produced the issue to assist with troubleshooting.
